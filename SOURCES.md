# Sources & Provenance

## Emission factors

Primary numerical source: UK Government, Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, **Greenhouse gas reporting: conversion factors 2026**.

CarbonGuard uses the revised July 2026 flat-file factor data in its deterministic factor service.

Official source page:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greenhouse-gas-reporting-conversion-factors-2026

## Retrieval representation

The CarbonGuard Knowledge Base contains a retrieval-oriented representation of the official factor dataset. The GOV.UK dataset remains authoritative.

## Application evidence

Observed test cases are documented in `TEST_RESULTS.md`; no unmeasured accuracy or latency claims are presented as benchmark facts.
